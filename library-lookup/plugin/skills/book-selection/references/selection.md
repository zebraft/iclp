# Selection details

Keep each original ID through normalization, comparison and export. Duplicate identifiers can flag records for review but do not authorize merging editions or removing records. If IDs are absent, assign sequential IDs once across the whole list, not separately per batch.

A scholarly comparison can use the user's interests, title, creator, publisher, year, edition and verified contents. Label inference and missing evidence. Do not invent reviews, prices, stock, cover art or a publication's academic standing. Local holdings are one decision factor, not an automatic rejection rule.

For exports larger than the tool's list bound, partition the supplied records without renumbering. Export selected IDs in their corresponding batches, track counts and failures, and return clearly named parts. Do not silently truncate to the first batch. Formula-safe CSV is computed data; no institutional destination or submission is implied.
